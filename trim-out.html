Finish list matched to the plan notes and the door and window schedules. Two floors, two kitchens, two bathrooms, two laundries. Levels are not connected by an interior stair. Flooring is wood-look LVP (note 8), 2,176 sqft. Siding is fiber cement board & batten, not T1-11 (note 4). Both showers are tiled (main 3'-8" × 6'-0" zero-threshold with bench, grab bars, and nonslip flooring; upper 4'-0" × 6'-0").

Pricing is mid-range standard from Home Depot Blue Ridge, GA area. Quantities estimated from 2,176 sqft living + porches. Materials only.
