Rough-In Materials List

Print this page (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) for a clean PDF.

Takeoff from the as-built plans (A101 / A102 / notes), Orndorff Residence, Ellijay GA. Each floor is 32'-0" × 34'-0" = 1,088 sqft. Overall with the 10'-0" porch is 44'-0" × 32'-0". The roof ridge on A102 runs the 34' direction, so the rafters span the 32' direction. Floor joists run the same way and bear on the north and south CMU walls: 27 joists at 32'-0" per floor (16" OC along 34'-0").

Main walls are 9'-0" (floor plan and cover sheet). Upper walls are 8'-0", with the 6/12 gable studs sitting on that wall — the rise is 8', so those studs are 8' or shorter, not 16'. Siding is fiber cement board & batten (note 4). Materials only. Add tax and delivery. No waste is added on the stud and joist counts; they are the piece count from the plan.
