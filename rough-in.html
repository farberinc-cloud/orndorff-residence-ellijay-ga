Rough-In Materials List

Print this page (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) for a clean PDF.

Takeoff from the as-built plans (A101 / A102 / notes), Orndorff Residence, Ellijay GA. Each floor is 32'-0" × 34'-0" = 1,088 sqft. Overall with the 10'-0" × 32'-0" porch is 44'-0" × 32'-0". Porches/decks are 664 sqft (320 + 320 + 24). Walls are 2×4 at 16" OC. Foundation is a CMU crawl space, so the floor stringers bear on those walls and are full length: 32' if they span the 32'-0" direction, or 36' stock if they span the 34'-0" direction. Same both floors. Roof is a 6/12 gable (A102) with a 12/12 entry-porch gable and a 1'-4" overhang.

Main-level ceilings are drawn and noted on the cover sheet at 9'-0". General note 12 says 8'-0" for the main level; the floor-plan labels and cover sheet are used here. Upper flat ceilings are 8'-0". Upper living, kitchen, bedroom, entry, and porch are vaulted on the roof pitch (notes 12–13). Siding is fiber cement board & batten, dark green (note 4) — not T1-11. No glulam is shown; the ridge is a 2× ridge board. Materials only. Add 10–15% waste, tax, and delivery. The 36' stringer row is an alternate layout, not added into the total.
