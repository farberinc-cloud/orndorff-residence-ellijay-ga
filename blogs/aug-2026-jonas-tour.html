August 2026: Jonas Brothers Announce Burning Up Tour All Over Again

August 17, 2026

In mid-August 2026, the Jonas Brothers expanded their plans from a special three-night stand at Madison Square Garden into a full 45-city North American tour titled The Burning Up Tour All Over Again.

The tour is a revival of their iconic 2008 Burning Up Tour, mixing classic set pieces and songs with newer material. It kicked off with sold-out shows at Madison Square Garden on August 20, 21, and 22, 2026, then continues across the U.S. and Canada through December 2026, wrapping in Newark, New Jersey.

Special guests on select dates include Magnus Ferrell, Franklin Jonas (the fourth Jonas brother), Deleasa, and All Time Low. Tickets went on sale starting with presales in mid-August and general sale on August 21.

Around the same time, Camp Rock 3 arrived on Disney+, bringing the brothers back to the franchise that helped launch their early fame. The combination of the tour announcement and the new film made August a major month for Jonas Brothers fans.

The brothers said the overwhelming response to the MSG dates made it clear they needed to take the show on the road so more fans could experience it. See the full schedule on our Jonas Brothers page (updated monthly).
