Notes, Abbreviations & Specifications

Extracted exactly from the as-built plan documents.

Abbreviations & Notation

  • 3068 - 3'-0"W x 6'-8"H
  • AFF - Above finished floor
  • AW - Awning window
  • BD - Sliding barn door
  • CO - Cased opening
  • DH - Double hung window
  • FD - Bi-folding door
  • FW - Fixed window
  • PD - Pocket door
  • SD - Sliding door
  • SH - Single hung window
  • TG - Tempered glass
  • TW - Transom window

Exterior Finishes

  • Siding material - Fiber cement board & batten, fiber cement trim, and a stone / ledgestone finish around the CMU block foundation walls. Exterior pressure treated wood features and columns are to be a dark natural stain.
  • Roofing material - Metal, in a charcoal/black color, or as otherwise specified by the owner.

Ceilings

  • Ceilings are to be vaulted following the roof pitch in the upper level living room, kitchen, bedroom, and porch. All other upper level ceilings shall be flat at 8'-0" a.f.f. All main level ceilings are to be flat at 9'-0" a.f.f.

Additional Notes

  • Exterior stairs and stoops may be modified to fit the final grade, as determined by the contractor on-site.
  • Square Footage: 1,088 sqft - Main Level | 1,088 sqft - Upper Level | 664 sqft - Porches/Decks
  • Note: Square footage on plans is shown as net sqft, by room.
  • Plans have been formatted for print on ArchD 24"x36" paper size. Drawing scale will not otherwise be accurate unless printed as such.
  • This plan set is specific to one lot/parcel/address and shall not be replicated for use on a separate build without the consent of the CAD drafter.
